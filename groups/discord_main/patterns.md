# User Patterns

Track recurring behaviors — both good and bad. Use these to give sharper advice.

## Positive Patterns
(What does the user do well? When is he at his best?)

## Warning Signs
(What does procrastination look like for the user? What excuses come up?)

## Decision-Making Style
(How does the user make decisions? Does he overthink? Act too fast? Need external validation?)

## Energy Cycles
(When is the user most productive? What drains him?)
